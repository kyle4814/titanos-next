What I build

Everything I describe on this site, I build and run myself, so I write about it from direct use. The pieces that are live on titanos.tech are:

  • A free external check of a business domain that reads public records only, including its email authentication settings, and that I run only on a domain the owner asks me to look at. See the scan and the exact method.
  • A fixed-price Privacy Act and Essential Eight engagement for small businesses preparing for the changes that start on 10 December 2026. See the compliance pack.
  • Recurring security and compliance monitoring. See the monitor.
  • AI implementation: I find the repetitive task eating a team's week and build the system that does it, without replacing anyone. See how an engagement works.

How I work

I diagnose, plan and scope each job myself. Claude Code, Anthropic's coding tool, does the keystroke-level build under my direction, and I decide what is allowed to ship and sign it off. I say that openly because a reader should know when AI helped produce a page, and because the person you deal with is accountable for it. If a number on this site is wrong, tell me and I will correct it and note the date.

My writing follows three rules. Every figure carries its method and date. Every legal or technical claim links to a primary source, such as the OAIC, the Australian Cyber Security Centre, legislation.gov.au or the relevant standard. And a modelled number is never presented as revenue or as a client result. Titanos is early in its life, and the about page says plainly where things stand.

What I write about

These are the areas I cover, each with a place to start:

  • AI automation for small business
  • Email authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)
  • Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles
  • Notifiable Data Breaches scheme
  • ACSC Essential Eight
  • External attack surface monitoring
  • Business email compromise
  • Trade business admin automation

If you meet an unfamiliar term, the glossary defines DMARC, SPF, DKIM, the Essential Eight, the Australian Privacy Principles, the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme and more, each with a primary source.
