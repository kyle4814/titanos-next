WHAT BLACK ICE ACTUALLY IS

A polished black surface, enormous depth underneath. That's the whole idea. Most "AI productivity" content is either empty branding or a prompt-hack thread. Black Ice is neither. It's a small set of reusable habits for seeing a situation clearly, cutting the noise out of it, and acting on what's left, with AI doing the keystroke work and a human holding the one decision that actually needs judgement.

Worth checking on your own week: how much of it is spent on work an AI could already do, versus the one call only you should be making? The language here borrows metaphor on purpose, ice, depth, stillness. The doctrine page says exactly where that's a metaphor and where it's a mechanism, and never dresses one up as the other.

THE TWELVE PRIMITIVES

The full loop, compressed to one line each. Detail on the field guide page.

  1. 01
    OBSERVE
    See what's actually happening before reacting to it.
  2. 02
    ORIENT
    Place it against the objective that actually matters right now.
  3. 03
    SUBZERO
    One quiet pass, state, signal, friction, lever, before acting.
  4. 04
    EXPAND
    Let the idea get bigger before you judge it.
  5. 05
    COMPRESS
    Turn what worked into one reusable rule.
  6. 06
    QUESTION
    Nothing is sacred. Inspect the assumption load-bearing the plan.
  7. 07
    RED-TEAM
    Attack it to make it stronger, not to win an argument.
  8. 08
    CHOOSE
    Pick the one move that matters most under real constraints.
  9. 09
    EXECUTE
    Ship the decision. Discussion has a deadline.
  10. 10
    MEASURE
    Confidence isn't evidence. Check what actually happened.
  11. 11
    RELEASE
    Let go of what didn't survive contact with reality.
  12. 12
    REPEAT
    The loop is the system. Run it again.
