## Finding log

Copy this block for each finding. Write facts with a source. Keep guesses in the inferred line. Include blank searches.

### Finding number:

- [ ] Observed (I saw it directly in a public source)
- [ ] Inferred (my reading of what it might mean)

Finding, in one plain sentence:
Source (exact register or page):
Date searched:
Search terms used:
Evidence saved (copy or dated screenshot, where stored):
Bears on our decision? (yes, no, unclear):
Category (red flag, noise, unclear):
Ordinary explanation, if any:
Question to put to the other party:
Next step (nothing, ask them, take advice):

### Wording check before you move on

- [ ] The line states what the source showed, not what I think of the people
- [ ] It could be defended by pointing at the source and the date
- [ ] Any guess is in the inferred line and framed as a question
- [ ] It does not use words such as fraud, scam or liar

### Blank search record

Source searched:
Date:
Terms used:
Result: nothing found (this does not mean the organisation is clean)

### Stop check

- [ ] Possible sanctions list match
- [ ] Insolvency notice or court matter affecting money owed or about to be paid
- [ ] Suspected fraud or identity misuse
- [ ] Large or hard to exit contract with a red flag

If any box above is ticked, pause and speak to a lawyer or accountant before going further.
