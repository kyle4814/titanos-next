## Ranked fixes (each count = rows in this bank that fail on that pattern today; counts overlap, union of A to D = 64 of the 68 failing rows)

1. **Carry the job and the place through a clarifier reply (session.js / chat.js): 27 rows.** A free-text answer to a clarifier ("tiles on it", "its leaking", "you pick", "how many coats") is read cold, so it hits a second clarifier ("Tiling: what do you need?"), a generic "what are you working on?", or the "three nearest jobs" card, and the room from turn 1 (bathroom, balcony, shower) is dropped. Patterns covered: answer-got-clarifier-loop, lost-the-room-from-turn-1, pick-asked-clarifier-instead, followup content misses. Kill test: the 27 rows (script-203, 212, 215, 216, 220, 221, 222, 227, 230, 231, 235, 236, followup-185, 186, 188, 189, 195, 196, 200 and others) pass on a rerun with unit_convo.mjs still green.
2. **Widen the not-found hole in the social and place lexicon: 15 rows.** Hits the not-published card: "lol ok", "why cant you just answer me", "arvo mate need a hand with a reno", "hey u there need help with the reno", nouns "fence", "stairs", "pergola", "footpath", "skylight", plus "dunno what i need for a wet room", "gluin timber to brick", "what is best for gluing a handrail", "ur products any good for a shed floor". Fix is lexicon only (slang: arvo, reno, u there, ur; place nouns; frustration phrases). The --gate counts the social subset (4 rows today: social-019, social-020, typo-105, typo-130).
3. **Turn unranked lists and non-pick answers into a "My pick" lead when the ask was a recommend: 14 rows.** 8 rows get a "whose published text documents a fit" list with no pick (e.g. "which grout for outdoor tiles", "which sealant for outdoor joints that move"), 6 get a product card or a training page with no "My pick" lead (e.g. "best construction adhesive for a workshop bench" returns the "Training from Sika Australia" info card, "tile adheseve for outside" returns a bare product card).
4. **Put clarifiers on the bare place nouns that currently get a list: 6 rows** ("metal roof", "gutter", plus 3 more vague rows and a typo row); one vague row has the wrong chip count (vague-037). Make the vague path win over the "match your words" advice list for a lone noun.
5. **Quantity without a product or thickness: 4 rows** ("how much would I need for 12 m2" offers a product picker; "how much for 30 m2" after a repair-mortar answer asks thickness in mm and loses the area just given; 3 of these 4 rows are also inside fix 1, so exclusive gain is about 1 row). Carry the stated area into the clarifier and ask for the one missing thing.

## Method notes and limits

- Pass/fail rules live in convo_score.mjs and are deliberately mechanical (card type, My pick lead, quoted source sentence, chip count, regex on the final answer). A pass is not proof the answer is right, only that it has the right shape; a fail can be a too-strict expectation (INFERRED, review the examples above before building to them).
- The not-found check reads the first card only (a comparison card legitimately says "not in the published documentation" for a silent column; the first draft of the scorer counted those 5 rows as failures, corrected before this baseline).
- Ties ("Two products tie") count as a valid pick answer, per unit_convo.mjs.
