## What changed and why
1. **Clarify (chat.js, session.js).** A vague ask gets one question, never a guessed product: a room or surface with no problem ("the kitchen bench", "outside wall", "my shower walls") asks the job for that place (new Bench place; two places named with no job word ask the more particular one instead of resolving a surface word into a tiling pick); a problem with no surface ("its peeling off", "it keeps lifting", "need to stick something down") asks what is coming away or being stuck (new Problem place; direction and state words are filler); two jobs in one message ("glue for tiles and a sealant for the bath") get "Two jobs there: which first?" with the catalogue's kind names as chips (twoJobs in chat.js, gated so a product tagline typed as a need is still a product search); typo'd pick asks ("wat shud i use", "wich one shud i get", "wut do u recomend") read; "what should I use" after an unrelated product turn asks the job (a job read off a product shown is marked implied and is not recommended on, except for a bare "recommend one"); a job verb beside a place ("sealing a gutter") takes that place's option. A pronoun after a fresh product ("what does it stick to") stays on the product. The inner brain's unknown on a message that names a kind ("the grout is going mouldy") falls back to a pick from that kind.
2. **Pick quality (brain.js, advisor.js).** A place the tile tree has no branch for (pool, spa, underwater, fire, heated, pond) is answered by the advisor, so "best tile adhesive for a pool" quotes a sentence that says pools. The wood floor kind prefers flooring adhesives (SikaBond T-55 J, SikaBond-58 Wood Floor) over construction adhesives when the ask also names concrete. A new acoustic condition, so "acoustic sealant" is judged on evidence (none in the data: no documented fit) instead of listing every sealant.
3. **Coverage gate, both ways (cover.js, brain.js).** False no fixed: a table row's colour cell is read ("available colour | light grey, ..." for Sarnafil Metal PVC, "Colour (CQP001-1) | Black" for Sikaflex-268); "special primer / special prep / special trowel" is not "on special"; "delivers" is not delivery; "sikabond 55", "sikafloor 32", "sikasil 25" resolve to the real product instead of "there is no SikaBond-55"; "anti slip paint" and floor/epoxy paint reach the coatings; "slip joint" and "slip sheet" are not slip ratings; the carbon attribute matches carbon footprint words, never "carbon fibre" in a name. False yes closed: a short message that names a kind or category noun after a product ("fire rated sealant", "grout colours", "cheapest tile adhesive") is a new ask of the range, never the held product; price, stock and delivery asked of a KIND with no product named ("cheapest grout") get the honest no for that kind with no product card.
4. **Quantity edge cases (text.js, session.js).** "3 x 4 m2" is 12 m2 (was 4), "2400 x 1200 mm" is 2.88 m2, "2 x 3m joints" is 6 m of joint (never 6 m2); a bare "12", "3 x 4" or "about 3 by 4 metres" answers the area question for the product held; "and at 25 m2" and "same for SikaTile-400 EVO PRO" (held area, another product) work.
5. **Compare (chat.js).** "which one should i buy, X or Y" strips the lead; "and versus X?" and "how does X compare" after a product compare the held product with X.
