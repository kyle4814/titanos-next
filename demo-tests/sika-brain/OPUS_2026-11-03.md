## What changed and why
1. **cover.js (new) + brain.js coverAsk, wired first in session.run.** Before routing, the brain works out the asked OBJECT (named product, product held from the last turn, a model number, a thing outside the range) and ATTRIBUTE (fire rating, slip rating, food contact, drinking water, acoustic, warranty, Green Star, EPD, carbon, R value, origin, asbestos, VOC, colours, a standard by code, the NCC, price/stock/delivery, permits/legal/insurance), then checks the product's own text (product page + data sheet numbers, never the SDS boilerplate) for it. Absent: one not-in-the-data card that names exactly that pair ("The Sikaflex-11 FC+ data sheet and product page do not state a fire rating..."), lists what the sheet does cover, and offers chips for that product. A model number no product carries ("Sikaflex-999") gets "There is no Sikaflex-999 in the range". Timber, plasterboard, tiles, paint, fixtures, hardware get a plain "not in the range" plus what it can help with. Present but routed without the words: the answer is replaced by the product's own sentences that hold them (cover:quote, e.g. food contact for 11 FC+).
2. **The not-in-the-data card never carries a product** (cards.js unknown): the product moves to `about`, so no honest no reads as a product answer, and the session still holds the product for the next turn. Test tools that read the declined product (coverage/judge.mjs, sika-brain2c/conv.mjs) now read `product || about`; no expectation was loosened.
3. **Near misses kept answered:** colours with no colour list say so and show the published pack sizes; "available in a 15 L pail" shows the pack sizes (4 L and 20 L); a published warranty page still answers ("warranty for a tiling job with davco k10").
4. **Quantity:** "how much X do I need" with no area asks the one missing variable (the area); a bare "10 m2" or "about 15 square metres at 5mm" after that question computes; room sizes "3 by 4 metre" and "2.4m x 1.2m" read as 12 m2 and 2.88 m2 (text.js normalise); after a My pick, "and for 30 m2 how much" works it for the product picked, not the whole kind again.
5. **Pick and compare:** "good X for Y" and "what's the go for Y" read as pick asks; "floorboards" reaches the wood floor adhesive kind; "X or Y for <job>" comparisons carry "the data sheets do not rank products".
