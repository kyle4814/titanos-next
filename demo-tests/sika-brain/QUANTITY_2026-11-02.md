# QUANTITY ASKS FROM A PUBLISHED COVERAGE TABLE: before and after, 2026-11-02

Branch sika/quantity-2026-11-02, base 2e8900c64. Local rule engine only, no model, no network. Reproduce: node demo-tests/sika-brain/unit_grid.mjs (28 checks; 19 fail on 2e8900c64).

## What changed

The quantity card used to refuse any coverage table row it could not read as 'a rate per area' ('I will not turn this into a quantity: the published figure does not say what it is per'). Davco Easy Grout's table does say: its basis line is 'Grout coverage based on 2 Litre pail', its columns are 'TILE SIZE | 1.5mm Joint | 3mm Joint | 5mm Joint'. The brain now reads that basis (assets/brain/grid.js, called from cards.js calcCard):

- tile size and joint width given: the cell is computed, arithmetic and the quoted row shown, packs rounded up in every published pack size of the same unit.
- one of them missing: a small result table across the published options (all joints for that tile, or all tiles for that joint), with chips to pick one.
- neither given: ONE question (tile size) with a chip per table row, each carrying the area.
- tile or joint not in the table: no figure is invented, the published options are offered.
- a figure that fails the data check (calc flag false, e.g. Superfine 10x50x6mm 0.8 kg) is not computed.
- tables with no stated pack basis (SikaTile-510 Mega Lite: 'Approximate coverage' only) stay the honest refusal.
- notched-trowel tables headed 'per bag' (SikaTile-405/366/374/350) now show each trowel size labelled with its own working (they used to print two unlabelled quantities).

## Arithmetic (all from published cells)

- Easy Grout 300x100x10mm, 3mm joint: 20 m2 / 7 m2 per 2 L pail = 2.857, rounded up to 3 pails = 6 L. In 4 L pails (Bright white only, per the packaging line): 5.71 L needs 2 pails.
- Superfine White Grout 200x200x6mm, 3mm: 20 m2 x 0.4 kg per m2 = 8 kg.
- SikaCeram-690 Elite 200x200x8mm, 6mm: 20 m2 x 1.2 kg per m2 = 24 kg = 5 x 5 kg bags.
- SikaTile-366 Pro Flex, 10 mm trowel: 20 m2 / 8 m2 per 20 kg bag = 2.5, rounded up to 3 bags.

## Tables made computable (56 products carry a headed consumption table; ask: 'How much <product> for 20 m2')

- Before 2e8900c64: 7 give a quantity card, 49 refuse.
- After: 7 quantity cards (4 of them the trowel tables, now labelled), 4 ask the ONE missing question and then compute (Davco Easy Grout, Davco Superfine White Grout, Davco Sanitized Colorgrout, SikaCeram-690 Elite), 45 stay honest refusals. So computable tables go 7 -> 11 (+4 grout tables; 50 of their 52 tile x joint cells compute: Easy Grout 9/9, Sanitized Colorgrout 14/14, SikaCeram-690 Elite 19/19, Superfine 8/10, the 2 left out being the figures that fail the data check).
- Still refused, and why: layer/coating-system tables (per layer, mixed coats), joint-length-per-pack sealants (need a joint length, not an area), yield-per-bag tables (volume, not area), dosage tables, tables with no stated basis (e.g. SikaTile-510, 440, 410). WOULD-FLIP: a table whose header names the pack or the unit per m2.

## Quantity asks to the quote cart (branch sika/qty-cart-2026-11-02, base 22036a3ee), 2026-11-02

Local rule engine and the browser only: no model, no network. Reproduce: `node unit_cart.mjs` (18 checks; the file fails to load on 22036a3ee, there is no tableAsk and no card.cart), `cart_e2e.mjs` (browser, Windows node.exe, serve_wsl.mjs; screenshots in shots_cart/).

### 1. Product-less asks now ask WHICH product
- "how much grout for 20 m2": before, one grout (Davco Rejuvenation Grout, 1.7 kg) was listed and four grid grouts were named under "Left out ... (no usable published rate)". Now the same card says which products publish a table that depends on tile size and joint width, with a chip each (Davco Superfine White Grout, SikaCeram-690 Elite, Davco Easy Grout, Davco Sanitized Colorgrout). Tap one and it asks the tile (chips from the table rows), then the joint (chips), then computes. The four are no longer listed as "left out".
- "how many bags of tile adhesive for 15 m2": the existing ranked list stays (existing test wave 2b needs it) and the SikaTile trowel-table products that appear in the group get a chip each; tapping asks the notched trowel size, then computes. The chip set is the group's picks for that question, so it differs by question (3 chips for "how many bags ... for 15 m2", 8 for "... of 600x600 tiles").
- When a group has chips and nothing computes outright, the answer is a plain choose card.
- Chips on calculation cards are now drawn by the panel (brain-ui.js). Before, the follow-ups on a calculation card (for example the joint-width options under a tile-only grout answer) were in the card data but not drawn as buttons.
- Sealant asks (per mm of thickness) are untouched: they still ask the thickness.

### 2. "Add 3 x 2 L pails to my quote"
- A computed answer carries card.cart: one option per published pack size (at most two), label built from the answer's own pack count. Easy Grout 300x100x10mm, 3 mm joint, 20 m2: 20 / 7 = 2.86, rounded up to 3, so "Add 3 x 2 L pails to my quote". The 4 L pail (Bright white only) is not offered as a cart option, since it is not available in every colour. Superfine 8 kg offers 6 x 1.5 kg and 2 x 5 kg. SikaTile-366, 10 mm trowel: 3 x 20 kg bags.
- Tile-only or joint-only answers (a table across options) and refusals carry no cart option: there is no single answer to add yet.
- The button (assets/lib/cartbtn.js) calls the existing project store addItem (lib/projectstore.js, key sika-demo-210c0c-project-v2): qty 3, unit pack, packs "3 x 2 L pail", need "5.71 L", the job, the data sheet link and the quoted row. Pressing it twice keeps one line (the store merges by product), so it never becomes 6 pails. No new storage.
- Browser check (cart_e2e.mjs, 390 and 1366 px): bare grout ask, Easy Grout chip, 300x100x10mm chip, 3 mm joint chip, the answer, the button, then My projects and quotes shows Davco Easy Grout, 3 packs, "packs: 3 x 2 L pail". Zero console errors, no horizontal scroll.

### 3. Computable tables: 11 before, 11 after (no new one)
Every other headed consumption table was read against the same test (the table's own header states what the figure is per, and the data check passes). None qualifies, so none was added; nothing was loosened to reach a bigger number.
- SikaTile-410, 440, 510: the header says "Approximate coverage" with no pack. Refusal stands. WOULD-FLIP: a header or sentence naming the pack.
- Sealant and foil-pack tables (Sikaflex, SikaHyflex, Sikasil Pool, SikaSwell): metres of joint per pack, so they need a joint length, not an area. A different question shape.
- Yield-per-bag grouts (Lanko Duragrout, Sikagrout 212, HES): a volume per bag, not an area.
- Sikafloor and Sikagard layer or coating-system tables: per layer, mixed coats.
- CarboDur, FloorJoint: per metre of plate or joint.
The 3 plain-rate tables (3 + 4 grid grouts + 4 trowel tables = 11) already compute outright and are unaffected.
