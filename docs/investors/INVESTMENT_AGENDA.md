# TitanOS Investment Agenda

## Objective

Create an investment-ready path for TitanOS that funds the existing autonomous engineering system and a new evidence-verification product line without inventing traction, valuation, eligibility, revenue or investor interest.

## Investment thesis to investigate

TitanOS can potentially become a platform around:

1. Autonomous software operations — persistent work queues, verification, receipts and bounded agent execution.
2. Evidence verification — a portal that helps users distinguish verified facts, supported claims, contradictions and unknowns.
3. AI reliability — tooling that makes hallucination, provenance and model-vs-reality boundaries explicit.
4. Opportunity intelligence — evidence-backed discovery and tracking of grants, programs and other funding opportunities.
5. Investor intelligence — a continuously maintained evidence room for product, traction, economics and capital planning.

These are hypotheses to validate, not established market facts.

## Capital workstreams

### A. Non-dilutive funding
- Grants and public programs.
- Open-source/security programs.
- Founder-accessible international opportunities.
- Maintain canonical source, eligibility, deadline, requested amount and verification date.

### B. Angel / pre-seed
Build an evidence-backed investor room containing:
- problem statement;
- product demo;
- architecture;
- customer/problem evidence;
- current traction;
- revenue and cost evidence;
- roadmap;
- use of funds;
- capital requirements;
- risks and mitigations;
- founder information;
- financing assumptions.

Unknowns remain UNKNOWN.

### C. Strategic capital
Investigate angels, operators, security/AI investors, open-source sponsors and strategic partners only after defining a factual fit model.

## New product investment case

The Truth Portal should be treated as a distinct product thesis inside the TitanOS portfolio.

### Problem hypothesis

People increasingly receive information from humans, search systems, AI models and simulations whose provenance and certainty are unclear. Existing workflows can collapse:

- "someone said it"
- "a source reported it"
- "an AI inferred it"
- "a simulation predicts it"
- "we verified it"

into one undifferentiated statement.

### Product hypothesis

A verification layer can preserve those distinctions and produce an auditable evidence graph rather than another opaque answer.

### Validation before capital claims

The autonomous system should seek evidence for:
- target users;
- high-value verification jobs;
- willingness to pay;
- existing alternatives;
- technical feasibility;
- retrieval/source quality;
- false-positive and false-negative risk;
- privacy/security requirements;
- unit economics.

## Use-of-funds template

| Bucket | Purpose | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Product | Portal + verification engine | engineering plan |
| Infrastructure | retrieval, storage, compute | cost model |
| Security | privacy, abuse controls, auditability | threat model |
| Distribution | pilots and acquisition | test results |

| Operations | accounting/admin | actual forecast |

## Human gates

The system may research, structure and draft investment materials.

The human retains final control over:
- whether to raise;
- amount sought;
- valuation;
- securities/terms;
- investor selection;
- representations made to investors;
- grant submissions;
- legal/tax commitments.

## Evidence rule

Every investor-facing factual claim must carry:
- source;
- date/period;
- evidence location;
- verification state;
- owner;
- last checked date.

No unsupported "huge market", "winner", "guaranteed", "best", "fully autonomous", or traction claim enters the canonical investor room.
