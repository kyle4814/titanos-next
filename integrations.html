Trade Software Automation Guides: ServiceM8, Tradify, simPRO, Fergus and Jobber

By Kyle Deligny, Titanos, Brisbane. Last reviewed 28 November 2026.

A small trades business loses work in four quiet places: the call nobody answered, the quote nobody chased, the happy customer nobody asked for a review, and the invoice nobody reminded. The job software you already pay for may do some of these itself. The rest usually needs a connector such as Zapier or Make, or the vendor's API.

These five guides sort that out for ServiceM8, Tradify, simPRO, Fergus and Jobber. For each job they show what the software does natively, what needs Zapier, Make or the API, and the exact steps, with a link to the vendor's own page for every claim. Each also carries a worked example whose numbers are labelled as examples.

Based on each vendor's documentation as at November 2026. We have not tested these integrations ourselves. Vendors change plans, names and limits, so confirm each step in your own account before you rely on it.

Three patterns that repeat across all five

First, the follow-up jobs (quotes, invoices, reviews) are the ones vendors most often build in, because they depend only on data the software already holds. Second, missed-call text-back is almost never built into job software. It depends on your phone system, so the connector's job is to log the caller in your software, not to send the text. Third, anything running through Zapier or Make adds a second place where messages can fail, so test every automation on your own phone and number before real customers see it.

Rules that apply whichever software you use

  • Commercial texts are covered by the Spam Act 2003. Check consent, identification and the way to opt out.
  • Customer phone numbers are personal information. Collect them for a stated purpose and keep them where your team already keeps customer data.
  • One owner for each automation. If nobody checks the list each morning, an automated lead is only a faster way to lose it.
  • ACMA: avoid sending spam (Spam Act 2003)
  • OAIC: privacy for businesses

Frequently asked questions

Which trade software has the most built-in follow-up automation?

From the vendor pages we read as at November 2026, Jobber and ServiceM8 document built-in quote, invoice and review follow-ups. Tradify documents quote and invoice reminders. simPRO uses Notifications. Fergus relies on Zapier.

Have you tested these integrations?

No. Every guide is based on the vendor's own documentation, API reference and marketplace pages, and says so. Treat any Zapier, Make or API route as a plan to trial before you rely on it.

Can I text customers automatically in Australia?

Commercial messages are covered by the Spam Act 2003. Read the ACMA guidance on consent and identification before you automate anything beyond replying to someone who has just contacted you. This is general information, not legal advice.

Where to go next
