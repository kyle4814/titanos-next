Jobber Zapier Guide: Reviews, Quotes and Invoice Follow-Up

By Kyle Deligny, Titanos, Brisbane. Last reviewed 28 November 2026.

Jobber is the one of the five that advertises the full set of chasing jobs on its own features page: automated follow-ups for pending quotes and unpaid invoices, and Google review requests after every job. What its page does not describe is a missed-call feature, and that is where Zapier or Make comes in.

This guide is based on Jobber's documentation and marketplace pages as at November 2026. We have not tested these integrations ourselves. Jobber's features page does not state timing options or plan requirements for its automations, so we give what the pages say and send you to Jobber's pricing page for the rest.

Based on Jobber's documentation as at November 2026. We have not tested these integrations ourselves. Vendors change plans, names and limits, so confirm each step in your own account before you rely on it.

Is Jobber the right fit for this?

Jobber suits you here if you want the least setup: three of the four jobs are built in and the fourth has a documented connector. Make also lists a Jobber app that it describes as supported and maintained by the vendor.

Missed-call text-back with Jobber

What Jobber does natively

Jobber's automations page does not describe a missed-call or missed-text automation. It mentions appointment reminders and on-my-way texts, which are different.

What needs Zapier, Make or the API

Use your phone provider for the text-back and a Zap or Make scenario to log the enquiry. Zapier's Jobber app has actions named Create Client, Create Request and Add Note to Request, and a Find Client action. Make lists a Jobber app that is supported and maintained by Jobber, and its page describes adding new leads into Jobber and sending SMS or email notifications.

Exact steps

  1. Set up missed-call text-back in your phone provider and read its documentation for the trigger.
  2. In Zapier, add a Zap with that trigger, then the Jobber action Find or Create Client so a repeat caller is not duplicated.
  3. Add Create Request with the phone number and "Missed call" in the details, so it lands in your Requests list.
  4. Test with your own phone and check the request appears once.
  5. Read the Jobber help centre on requests and decide who checks the list each morning.
Sources:
  • Jobber on Zapier: triggers and actions
  • Make: Jobber app
  • ACMA: avoid sending spam (Spam Act 2003)

Quote follow-up in Jobber

What Jobber does natively

Jobber's pages describe automated follow-ups for pending quotes by email or text that remind the client to review and approve. Jobber's wording on timing varies by page, with one saying you can choose when to send reminders, from an hour after a client views a quote to several days later. The page we read does not list plan requirements.

What needs Zapier, Make or the API

Not needed. If you want a sequence in another system, Zapier's Jobber app has New Quote Created, Quote Sent and Quote Approved triggers.

Exact steps

  1. Open Jobber's automations settings and find the quote follow-up option.
  2. Choose email, text or both and set the timing to match how fast your customers decide.
  3. Edit the wording so it sounds like you, with one clear next step.
  4. Check Jobber's pricing page to confirm the automation is included on your plan.
  5. Send a quote to your own address and confirm the follow-up stops once you approve it.
Sources:
  • Jobber: automations
  • Jobber on Zapier

Review requests in Jobber

What Jobber does natively

Jobber's automations page says it can automatically send Google review requests after every job. The page does not list timing options.

What needs Zapier, Make or the API

To use a separate review tool, Zapier's Jobber app has a Job Closed trigger, and Jobber's own Academy article on Zapier lists job completions among its triggers. Zapier also lists templates that send review requests to third-party review tools when a Jobber invoice or quote changes.

Exact steps

  1. Open Jobber's automations settings and switch on review requests.
  2. Follow Jobber's own prompts to add your Google review link.
  3. Check what the message says and whether it goes by email or text.
  4. If you use another review tool, create a Zap on Job Closed with a Delay and that tool's action instead, and turn off Jobber's version so customers are not asked twice.
Sources:
  • Jobber: automations
  • Jobber Academy: Jobber and Zapier integrations

Invoice reminders in Jobber

What Jobber does natively

Jobber's pages describe automated follow-ups for unpaid invoices, sent as email or text, reminding clients to pay. The page does not specify the schedule.

What needs Zapier, Make or the API

Not needed for a basic reminder. Zapier's Jobber app lists New Invoice Created and Invoice Updated triggers if you want an alert in Slack or a spreadsheet.

Exact steps

  1. Open the automations settings and switch on the invoice follow-up.
  2. Read the schedule and the message Jobber will send.
  3. Match your payment terms, and keep the tone friendly in the first reminder.
  4. Spot-check one real overdue invoice a week for the first month.
Sources:
  • Jobber: automations
  • Jobber on Zapier

What we could not confirm

  • Jobber's developer documentation blocked our automated reader, so we make no claim about its API or webhooks beyond what Make and Zapier list.
  • Jobber's automations page does not state timing options or plan requirements, and one source's timing conflicts with a Zapier template's fixed delay. Confirm both in your account.
  • We found no built-in missed-call feature, which is an observation about the pages we read.

Before you text customers automatically

Commercial text messages in Australia are covered by the Spam Act 2003, and customer phone numbers are personal information. Read the guidance first. This is general information, not legal advice.

  • ACMA: avoid sending spam (Spam Act 2003)
  • OAIC: privacy for businesses

Frequently asked questions

Does Jobber send Google review requests automatically?

Jobber's automations page says it can automatically send Google review requests after every job. It does not list timing options.

Is there a Jobber Zapier integration?

Yes. Zapier lists a Jobber app with eleven triggers, such as New Quote Created, Quote Approved, New Invoice Created and Job Closed, and eight actions.

Can Jobber do missed-call text-back?

We found no built-in missed-call feature. The documented route is a phone provider plus a Zapier or Make scenario that creates a Jobber request.
