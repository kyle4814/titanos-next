ServiceM8 Automation for Tradies: Texts, Quotes, Reviews

By Kyle Deligny, Titanos, Brisbane. Last reviewed 28 November 2026.

ServiceM8 is unusual among trade software because most of what a small business wants chased is already a built-in automation: quote follow-ups, payment follow-ups and feedback requests all live in its Automation add-on. The gap is the front of the funnel. ServiceM8's own help index shows no missed-call feature, so that one needs a phone tool and an integration.

This guide separates the two. For each of the four jobs you get what ServiceM8 does natively, what needs Zapier, Make or the API, and the steps to set it up, each with a link to ServiceM8's own page. Everything is based on ServiceM8's documentation as at November 2026. We have not tested these integrations ourselves, so treat the Zapier and API routes as a build plan to verify in a trial, not as a recipe that is known to work.

Based on ServiceM8's documentation as at November 2026. We have not tested these integrations ourselves. Vendors change plans, names and limits, so confirm each step in your own account before you rely on it.

Is ServiceM8 the right fit for this?

ServiceM8 suits you here if you already pay for the Automation add-on, because three of the four jobs need no extra tool. The most useful thing to do first is the Quote Follow Up automation, since it is built in, takes minutes, and acts on money you have already quoted.

Missed-call text-back in ServiceM8

What ServiceM8 does natively

We found no missed-call feature in the ServiceM8 help-centre index we read. The nearest native piece is the SMS you can send from inside a job once it exists, which is not triggered by a call.

What needs Zapier, Make or the API

Use a phone or VoIP service that can fire an action on a missed call and send the text itself. Zapier lists ServiceM8 pairings with several phone apps, and ServiceM8's Zapier app has actions named Create Client and Create Job, so a Zap can log the caller as a client or job once the phone tool reports the call. Make also has a ServiceM8 app that is supported by Make. Alternatively, ServiceM8's Messaging API sends email and SMS, but only from a Public Application using OAuth 2.0, with a daily sending quota and a maximum send rate, and some scopes such as publish_sms may incur account charges.

Exact steps

  1. In your phone or VoIP provider, find its missed-call or call-ended trigger. Read its documentation to confirm the trigger exists and what it passes on (caller number, time).
  2. Set the provider to send the text-back itself, so the customer hears from you within a minute, for example: "Sorry we missed you, we are on a job. Reply with your suburb and what you need and we will call you back today."
  3. In Zapier, create a Zap with the phone provider as the trigger and ServiceM8 as the action. Choose Create Client first, then, if you want a job card, add Create Job.
  4. Map the caller number into the client's mobile field and put "Missed call" and the time in the job description so your office person sees where it came from.
  5. Test with your own phone before you turn it on, and check ServiceM8 afterwards for duplicate clients if the same person rings twice.
Sources:

  • ServiceM8 Messaging API overview

Built in

Quote follow-up in ServiceM8

What ServiceM8 does natively

The Quote Follow Up automation emails and/or texts clients after you send a quote. You choose up to 5 follow-ups, pick email, SMS or both for each, and set a delay for each, such as an SMS 3 hours later and an email 3 days later. It can also mark jobs with unaccepted quotes as Unsuccessful after a timeframe you set. It needs the Automation add-on, which ServiceM8 says is available on all plans, and each SMS uses a text credit from your plan.

What needs Zapier, Make or the API

Nothing external is needed. Zapier's ServiceM8 app lists a Job Queued and a New Job trigger, which could feed a separate CRM, but the built-in automation already cancels itself when the customer replies, accepts online, or you move the job to Work Order, Completed or Unsuccessful.

Exact steps

  1. Open Settings > ServiceM8 Add-ons and make sure Automation is activated.
  2. Go to Settings > Automation and click Edit next to Quote Follow Up.
  3. Choose how many follow-ups to send (the maximum is 5).
  4. For each one, pick email, SMS or both, and set its delay after the quote is sent.
  5. Write the template for each message. ServiceM8's Smart Assistant can generate or improve a draft; read it before saving.
  6. Optionally turn on the status update that marks unaccepted quotes Unsuccessful, and set the timeframe.
  7. Send a real quote to a contact on the job. The scheduled messages appear in the job's Diary, where a team member can also cancel them.
Sources:
  • ServiceM8: Quote Follow Up Automation
  • ServiceM8: Automation overview

Partly built in

Review requests in ServiceM8

What ServiceM8 does natively

ServiceM8 has a Customer Feedback add-on. Its help page says it collects feedback through a smart link you place in email signatures, your website or other materials; the customer gives a star rating and can write a short review. The Automation add-on also lists a Customer Feedback automation that requests feedback after a job invoice is paid, by text, email or both, and it needs the Customer Feedback add-on. The pages we read do not mention a Google review link.

What needs Zapier, Make or the API

If your goal is Google reviews specifically, the documented route is outside ServiceM8: ServiceM8's Zapier app has a Job Completed trigger, so a Zap can wait, then send your own text through an SMS app with your Google review link in it. We have not confirmed whether ServiceM8's own feedback message can carry a Google link, so check that before you build the Zap.

Exact steps

  1. Turn on the Customer Feedback add-on in your add-ons settings, then open the Customer Feedback automation from Settings > Automation.
  2. Choose text, email or both and read the message that will go out.
  3. To aim at Google instead, create a Zap: trigger ServiceM8 Job Completed, add a Delay step (for example one day), then an action in your SMS or email tool.
  4. Put your Google review link and the customer's first name in the message, and keep it one short sentence with one ask.
  5. Test on a job of your own and confirm only completed, paid jobs get the message.
Sources:
  • ServiceM8: what the Customer Feedback add-on is for
  • ServiceM8: Automation overview
  • ServiceM8 on Zapier

Built in

Invoice reminders in ServiceM8

What ServiceM8 does natively

The Payment Follow Up automation requests payment from clients with overdue accounts by text, email or both. It is timed from when the job status changed to Completed and is sent only if the invoice is unpaid. Live Invoice Statements is a second option: it sends a client a statement of outstanding invoices weekly, every two weeks or monthly, enabled per client.

What needs Zapier, Make or the API

Not needed for the basic job. If you want reminders to reach another system, ServiceM8's webhooks can notify your own endpoint when a Job Payment object changes, but the payload lists only the changed field names and you must call the resource URL for values. Public OAuth apps must complete a subscription challenge.

Exact steps

  1. Confirm Automation is on under Settings > ServiceM8 Add-ons.
  2. In Settings > Automation, open Payment Follow Up and use its wizard.
  3. Set the delay from Completed to match your payment terms, so a client on 14-day terms is not chased on day 3.
  4. Choose channels and write the message: friendly first, firmer later, with your bank or payment link.
  5. For regular account customers, enable Live Invoice Statements per client and pick weekly, fortnightly or monthly.
Sources:
  • ServiceM8: Automation overview
  • ServiceM8 API: webhooks overview

What we could not confirm

  • We did not find an official ServiceM8 page describing a missed-call text-back feature.
  • We could not confirm that ServiceM8's own feedback message can include a Google review link.
  • ServiceM8's page states that webhooks are listed as a benefit of OAuth public applications and says API-key requests skip the subscription challenge, but does not say plainly whether an API key can receive webhooks. Ask ServiceM8 before you build on it.

Before you text customers automatically

Commercial text messages in Australia are covered by the Spam Act 2003, and customer phone numbers are personal information. Read the guidance first. This is general information, not legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

Does ServiceM8 have missed-call text-back built in?

We found no such feature in ServiceM8's help-centre index. The documented routes use a phone provider plus Zapier, Make or the ServiceM8 API, which we have not tested.

Do I need Zapier to chase quotes in ServiceM8?

No. The Quote Follow Up automation is built in, supports up to 5 follow-ups by email, SMS or both, and needs the Automation add-on.

Does ServiceM8 send Google review requests automatically?

ServiceM8 documents a Customer Feedback add-on and automation, but the pages we read do not mention a Google review link. A Zap on the Job Completed trigger is the documented route for sending your own message.
