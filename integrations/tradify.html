Tradify Zapier and Automation Guide: What Is Built In

By Kyle Deligny, Titanos, Brisbane. Last reviewed 28 November 2026.

People search for "tradify zapier" expecting a connector. In the sources we checked there is none: we found no Tradify listing on Zapier or Make, and Tradify's integrations page lists Xero, QuickBooks and Stripe only. That is a finding, not a failure of the software, because Tradify builds the two chasing jobs most owners want straight into the product.

So this guide is shorter on tools and longer on settings. It covers what Tradify does natively for quotes and invoices, the honest gap for missed calls and reviews, and one documented workaround that goes through your accounting package. It is based on Tradify's documentation as at November 2026, and we have not tested these integrations ourselves. Where a Tradify page was blocked to us or silent, we say so.

Based on Tradify's documentation as at November 2026. We have not tested these integrations ourselves. Vendors change plans, names and limits, so confirm each step in your own account before you rely on it.

Is Tradify the right fit for this?

Tradify suits you here if your priority is quote and invoice chasing and you are happy to use its built-in reminders. If you need missed-call text-back or review automation wired into Tradify itself, you will be running those beside it, not inside it.

Missed-call text-back with Tradify

What Tradify does natively

We found no missed-call feature in Tradify's help pages or features pages we could read, and no public API, Zapier app or Make app that would let an outside tool create a job in Tradify automatically.

What needs Zapier, Make or the API

The realistic route is to run text-back in your phone or VoIP service, which does not need Tradify at all, and enter genuine enquiries into Tradify by hand. If Tradify later publishes an API or connector, that is the day to automate the second half. Confirm with Tradify support before assuming either exists.

Exact steps

  1. Set up missed-call text-back in your phone provider or a standalone text-back tool, using its own documentation.
  2. Write the message to ask one question: "Sorry we missed you. What do you need and what suburb are you in?"
  3. Have the office person or you add real enquiries to Tradify as a new customer or job each morning.
  4. Ask Tradify support in writing whether a webhook, API or Zapier connection is available for your plan, and record the date of their answer.
  5. Check the Spam Act rules in the ACMA guidance below before you send any text that is more than a reply to the caller.
Sources:
  • Tradify: integrations (Xero, QuickBooks, Stripe)
  • ACMA: avoid sending spam (Spam Act 2003)

Quote follow-up in Tradify

What Tradify does natively

Tradify has quote reminders you switch on in Settings > Quotes, with an option to send text reminders as well as email. Tradify's help material describes reminders going out 7 and 14 days after the quote is sent and 3 days before it expires, stopping when the quote is accepted, declined or expires. Text messages are optional, are switched on under Settings > Subscription & Billing, are available in Australia, New Zealand and the United Kingdom, and carry a per-message charge on top of your subscription.

What needs Zapier, Make or the API

Not needed. Because we found no Tradify connector, an external chaser is not a documented option today.

Exact steps

  1. Open Tradify's help article "Turn on Quote Reminders" and follow it for your account.
  2. In Settings > Quotes, switch on email reminders, then choose Send Text Reminders if you want texts.
  3. To use texts, enable them under Settings > Subscription & Billing and read the per-message price for your country.
  4. Check the reminder wording and your business name before you send a live quote.
  5. Send a quote to yourself, confirm the reminder is scheduled, and then accept it to confirm the reminders stop.
Sources:
  • Tradify: Turn on Quote Reminders
  • Tradify: Understanding text messages

Not found in the documentation

Review requests with Tradify

What Tradify does natively

We found no review-request feature in the Tradify pages we could read.

What needs Zapier, Make or the API

One route is built on a documented fact: Tradify connects to Xero and QuickBooks, and both have apps on Zapier and Make. A Zap can watch your accounting package for a paid invoice and then send your review request. We have not confirmed the exact trigger names in those apps, so open the app in the Zapier editor and read its trigger list first.

Exact steps

  1. Confirm that your Tradify invoices sync to Xero or QuickBooks, using Tradify's integrations page.
  2. In Zapier or Make, create a scenario that triggers on a paid invoice in your accounting app. Check the trigger name in that app before building.
  3. Add a short delay, then an action in your SMS or email tool.
  4. Message the customer with one sentence and your Google review link.
  5. If you do not use the accounting route, put a saved reply on your phone and send it by hand when you mark a job paid. A manual habit is better than a broken automation.
Sources:
  • Tradify: integrations
  • Zapier app directory
  • Make integrations

Invoice reminders in Tradify

What Tradify does natively

Tradify's invoice reminder page says it will send emails or text messages that automatically follow up on unpaid invoices, and that you switch this on and it chases payment on any unpaid invoices sent from Tradify. The page does not state how often reminders go out or how to change the schedule, so read the schedule in your account.

What needs Zapier, Make or the API

Not needed.

Exact steps

  1. Open the reminder settings in your Tradify account and switch automatic payment reminders on.
  2. Read the schedule shown, since Tradify's public page does not give it.
  3. Decide whether texts are worth enabling for invoices; they carry a per-message charge.
  4. Check that your payment details and terms are on the invoice template, because the reminder only chases invoices sent from Tradify.
Sources:
  • Tradify: invoice reminder software
  • Tradify: Understanding text messages

What we could not confirm

  • We found no Tradify app on Zapier or Make and no public Tradify API in the sources we read. That is an observation about what we found, not proof that none exists.
  • Tradify's help centre blocked our automated reader on two articles. The quote reminder timings (7 and 14 days, and 3 days before expiry) came from a summary of those articles, so confirm them in your own account.
  • Tradify's invoice reminder page does not give a schedule.

Before you text customers automatically

Commercial text messages in Australia are covered by the Spam Act 2003, and customer phone numbers are personal information. Read the guidance first. This is general information, not legal advice.

  • ACMA: avoid sending spam (Spam Act 2003)
  • OAIC: privacy for businesses

Frequently asked questions

Does Tradify work with Zapier?

We found no Tradify app on Zapier in the sources we checked, and Tradify's integrations page lists Xero, QuickBooks and Stripe. Ask Tradify support before planning around Zapier.

Can Tradify send quote reminders automatically?

Yes. Tradify documents quote reminders by email with an optional text reminder, switched on in Settings > Quotes.

Does Tradify chase unpaid invoices?

Tradify says it can send emails or texts that follow up on unpaid invoices sent from Tradify. The public page does not give the schedule.
