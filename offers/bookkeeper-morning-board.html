Is this one actually for you? The next bit is a short, honest list.

Who it's for

  • Bookkeepers and small accounting practices serving owner-operators
  • Practices with roughly 15 to 60 small clients
  • Anyone tired of unpaid time spent checking each client by hand

What you get

  • One board for you and one card per client
  • Overdue items, a count of uncategorised transactions and odd items
  • A single list to review at 7am and decide who to call
  • Optional client cards forwarded under your name
  • A roll-up channel just for you

And how does it go from a yes to a finished job?

How it works

  1. Your clients consent to share a read-only export or CSV.
  2. We set up one card per client from a settings row.
  3. You review the board each morning and decide who to call.

Why TITANOS does it differently

One setup row per client on the same proven code, so 30 clients means 30 rows, not 30 projects.
