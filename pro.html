Leads

Built

Leads are export-only: you send from your own inbox, and a per-country rules check runs before every export. The check below shows allow or block for each row with the plain reason. The rules are a draft until a lawyer signs them off. Terms are on request.

DRAFT rules, pending legal sign-off. This check is not legal advice. It exports a file only. It does not send anything, contact anyone or look up any register.

The list below is an example with made-up people. Replace it with your own rows to check them.

Rule table: DRAFT v1: pending legal sign-off, not a legal opinion. 1 allowed, 3 held back.
  • EX-1 (email): ALLOW. Allowed by the draft rules for this country.
  • EX-2 (email): BLOCK. The consent on this row is not enough for this channel in the buyer's country.
  • EX-3 (email): BLOCK. This person has opted out, so the row is never exported.
  • EX-4 (phone): BLOCK. A required field is missing or not recognised, so the row is held back rather than guessed.
