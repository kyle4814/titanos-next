3 SUBSTRATE PREPARATION
3.1 General. Prepare substrates in accordance with the manufacturer's Product Data Sheet (March 2025, Version 01.02) and the requirements below. Inspect the substrate against these requirements before application.
3.2 Substrate quality.
    - All surfaces must be installed according to manufacturer’s instructions and relevant Australian Standard(s) and be structurally sound, dry, clean and free from movement, oil, grease, wax, sealers, curing compounds, release agents and any other loose or contaminating material. Thoroughly clean old surfaces, or new surfaces that have been treated, prior to tiling.
    - Deeply contaminated surfaces must be prepared/abraded to a clean, sound surface. Expose at least 80% of the original substrate of painted surfaces.
    - Substrates shall meet relevant deflection standards outlined in the relevant Australian Standards (AS3958).
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality)
3.3 Substrate quality - Concrete substrates.
    - New concrete shall be 7 days old and be left with a wood float finish.
    - Steel trowel finished concrete is generally acceptable if the concrete allows water penetration.
    - Concrete above 35MPa shall be mechanically prepared.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > CONCRETE SUBSTRATES)
3.4 Substrate quality - Cement board and sheeting. All boards and sheets must be fixed in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions and specifically designed for tiling.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > CEMENT BOARD AND SHEETING)
3.5 Substrate quality - Screeds & mortar beds.
    - Sand cement screeds must be installed as per relevant Australian Standards and left for a minimum 24 hours prior to tiling.
    - Screeds may need longer drying time in cold climates.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > SCREEDS & MORTAR BEDS)
3.6 Substrate quality - Tile on tile applications. Ensure all existing tiles are firmly fixed, loose or hollow tiles must be removed and the area repaired using a suitable Sika repair mortar. Existing tiles shall be adequately cleaned prior to the application of the primer. Existing tiles shall be primed with SikaTile 015 Prep n Prime and allowed to dry prior to tiling.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > TILE ON TILE APPLICATIONS)

4 APPLICATION
4.1 General. Apply in accordance with the manufacturer's Product Data Sheet (March 2025, Version 01.02) and the requirements below.
4.2 Application data.
    - Mixing ratio: 5 - 6 Litres of clean potable water per 20kg bag
    - Consumption: 20kg Bag of SikaTile®-400 EVO PRO covers approximately 6-7m² with a 10mm notched trowel.
    - Pot Life: 1.5 hours
    - Open Time: 30 minutes
    - Adjustability time: 20 minutes
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Information)
4.3 Applied product ready for use.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Information > Applied product ready for use)
4.4 Equipment.
    - Personal protective equipment
    - Mixing drill with stirring attachment
    - Clean mixing buckets
    - Trowels
    (Cite: Data sheet pages 2-3: Application Instructions > Equipment)
4.5 Mixing. Measure the recommended amount of clean potable water in a clean mixing pail, gradually add the SikaTile®-400 EVO PRO powder while mixing with the drill until it reaches a smooth lump free trowel-able consistency. Allow the mix to stand / slake for 5 minutes then restir for an additional minute. Remix without adding additional water during the adhesives pot life.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Mixing)
4.6 Application. Tiles shall be fixed in accordance with the relevant Australian Standards.
    - Ensure the back of tiles are from dust and contaminates.
    - Apply the adhesive with a notched trowel to the substrate to achieve minimum coverage requirement outlined in Australian Standards AS 3958.
    - Apply the adhesive in a single direction and avoid swirls in the adhesive.
    - Apply tiles into the wet adhesive and break the adhesive notches to fully bed the tile in the adhesive layer. Ensure adequate coverage outlined in the Australian Standards is achieved to both substrate and the back of tiles.
    - Clean any residual adhesive off the face of the tile and out of the grout joints while the adhesive is still wet.
    - Expansion joints shall be installed as per relevant Australian Standards.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Application)
4.7 Cleaning of equipment. Tools and equipment can be cleaned with water prior to the adhesive setting. Adhesive that has set will require mechanical removal.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Cleaning Of Equipment)
4.8 Limitations and important considerations.
    - Test product in an inconspicuous area first
    - Do not apply in temperatures above 35ºC or below 5ºC
    - Do not allow the adhesive to skin prior to laying the tiles. If the adhesive skins remove and dispose of it, then re-apply fresh adhesive
    - Not suitable for moisture sensitive stone, seek further advice from Sika technical support.
    - Not suitable for use for direct application over timber substrates. Apply a suitable screed or ceramic tile underlay over timber substrates.
    - Due to external influences efflorescence is beyond the control of the manufacturer
    - Setting times of the adhesive can be affected by site conditions, climate and could be faster or slower than specified.
    - For applications externally above 3 meters use in conjunction with shelving angles or mechanical fixing systems.
    - SikaTile®-400 EVO PRO is not suitable for fixing resin backed tile and stone. Prime resin back tiles with SikaTile 015 Prep n prime prior to fixing, and refer to product information and limitations on the product data sheet
    - Contact Sika Australia for uses not mentioned in this document
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Limitations Of Use)
4.9 Further documentation. It is essential to plan the area to be tiled, with the inclusion of movement joints, in accordance with the relevant Australian Standard . Failure to do so may result in drummy or lifting tiles. Make sure normal building or surface movement is accommodated by installing movement joints as follows:
    - Over movement joints in the substrate
    - At a junction between different substrate materials
    - At fixtures interrupting the tile surface e.g. columns
    - At internal vertical corners
    - Around the perimeter of the tiling system
    - At a maximum of 4.5m centres, in a grid pattern
    - Movement joints should be kept free from dirt and adhesive
    - All movement joints can be filled with a suitable Sika sealant and suitable backing rod or backing tape
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Further Documentation)
