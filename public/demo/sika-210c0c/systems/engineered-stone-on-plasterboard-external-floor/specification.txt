3 SUBSTRATE PREPARATION
3.1 General. Prepare substrates in accordance with the manufacturer's Product Data Sheet (September 2026, Version 02.01) and the requirements below. Inspect the substrate against these requirements before application.
3.2 Substrate quality.
    - All surfaces must be installed according to manufacturer’s instructions and relevant Australian Standard(s) and be structurally sound, dry, clean and free from movement, oil, grease, wax, curing compounds, release agents and any other loose or contaminating material.
    - Prior to application, remove all sharp protrusions, which may pierce the membrane
    - Any voids, potholes in the substrate must be appropriately filled up with a high strength mortar refer to Sika Monotop® mortars.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality)
3.3 Substrate quality - Concrete.
    - All new concrete slabs must have a wood float finish and be allowed to cure for 28 days. (refer to priming details for early aged concrete)
    - Old concrete must be cleaned with a strong commercial grade detergent or degreaser. Residue must then be thoroughly washed off with clean water. Allow the surface to dry for at least 24 hours
    - All laitance and efflorescence must be removed prior to application.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > Concrete)
3.4 Substrate quality - Cement based Renders & Screeds.
    - Renders and screeds should have a wood float finish and be allow to cure for 5-7. (refer priming details for early aged green screeds)
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > Cement based Renders & Screeds)
3.5 Substrate quality - Cement Boards and Sheets.
    - All boards and sheet shall be installed in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions and be specifically designed for tiling applications.
    - Screw and nail heads must be treated with a Sika® Neutral cure silicone or Sika® Fillet Joint.
    - All sheet joints, seams, penetrations, and wall floor junctions shall have a Sika® Neutral cure silicone, Sikaflex® Fillet Joint or Sika SealTape F flexible waterproofing bandage.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > Cement Boards and Sheets)
3.6 Substrate preparation - Substrate Priming Chart.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Preparation > Substrate Priming Chart)

    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Preparation > Early aged (green) screeds Sikalastic® Moisture Seal)
3.8 Substrate preparation - Static Cracks - not subject to movement.
    - Small hairline cracks, up to 1mm wide, may be filled by the first application of SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof
    - For cracks / joints wider than 1mm, a joint filler should be applied along the length of the crack prior to the application of SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Preparation > Static Cracks - not subject to movement)
3.9 Substrate preparation - Non Dymanic Cracks - less than 3mm subject to movement.
    - All cracks / joints, irrespective of their width, must be filled firstly with the bond breaker sealant. Then
    - 50mm wide polyethylene / polypropylene tape should be placed over the crack, ensuring it adheres to the surface. Apply the SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof over the tape as per installation instructions.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Preparation > Non Dymanic Cracks - less than 3mm subject to movement)
3.10 Substrate preparation - Bond Breaker. SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof has high extensibility and is designed for use with a 12 mm bond breaker, a bead of Sika® Neutral cure silicone, Sika® Fillet Joint or a suitable Sika® flexible waterproofing bandage. The sealant applied must be tooled off to form a 12 mm wide bond breaker. A bond breaker must be installed at areas subject to movement, wall/wall junction, wall/floor junction, sheet joints and seams, penetrations and where there is a change in the direction or substrate type.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Preparation > Bond Breaker)
3.11 Substrate preparation - Falls to Drains. All areas shall have falls in accordance with National Construction Code (NCC) requirements.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Preparation > Falls to Drains)

    - 1. SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof requires no mixing. Apply directly from the pail. Use a brush or roller to apply the first coat of SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof on the area to be waterproofed.
    - 2. Allow the first coat to dry for approximately 2-3 hours before applying the 2nd coat at 90° to the first coat. Ensure there are no imperfections on the waterproofing membrane.
    - 3. Apply a third coat if necessary or required to do so. The minimum dry film thinkness shall be 1.00mm
    - 4. Allow the final coat to dry for at least 8 hours prior to tiling application

    - 2. Allow the first coat to dry for approximately 2-3 hours before applying the 2nd coat at 90° to the first coat. Ensure there are no imperfections on the waterproofing membrane.
    - 3. Apply a third coatas (as additional wear layer for pedestall and paver application.
    - 4. The minimum dry film thickness for under pedestall and paver shall be 1.6mm
    - 5. Allow the final coat to dry for at least 24 hours prior to pedestall and paver application
    - Drying times are influence by factors beyond the control of the manufacturer, use drying times as a guide only.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Application Method / Tools > Application under Pedestall & Paver application)
4.7 Application method / tools - Flood testing (if required).
    - Allow SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof to dry and cure for a minimum 72 hours at 23°C and 50% relative humidity for standard applications, and 96 hours at 23°C and 50% relative humidity for paver and pedestall application.
    - Conduct flood test for 24 hours at max 50mm depth with water.
    - Inspect membrane for water loss, release water and allow area to dry prior prior to subsequent coatings
    - Repair membrane if required.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Application Method / Tools > Flood testing (if required))
4.8 Cleaning of equipment. Clean all tools and application equipment immediately after us with water. Once cured, residual material can only be removed mechanically.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Cleaning Of Equipment)

    - SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof is not suitable for applications in area of negative hydrostatic pressure or rising damp. Seek sika technical advice.
    - Delay applications if rain or inclement weather is imminent.
    - SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof must be applied at the recommended coverage rates.

    - Partical board flooring shall be adequately prepared with all contaminates removed and over sheeted with a suitable ceramic tile underlay or reinforced screed.

4.5 Instructions.
    - All Substrates shall be installed as per manufacturers instructions, fit for purpose and designed to handle the loads of a Tile or Stone system.
    - Substrate Deflection: All Substrates under loads must not exceed L/360 for tile and L/720 for Stone.
    - Substrates must be properly cured, structurally sound, free from loose or friable particles, clean, dry and free from contaminates such as dust, dirt, oil, grease, curing compounds, crystaline sealers, laitance, or effloresence.
    - Dependant on the substrate condition and contamination to be removed from the surface, perform adequate scarification, or blast cleaning in order to remove all traces of any materials that could reduce the product adhesion to the substrate.
    - Substrates shall be smooth and continuous without pit, divots. Repair pits or divots with a suitable Sika repair mortar prior to application.
    (Cite: Data sheet pages 3-4: Application Instructions)

    - Slow Speed Mixing Drill (Max 500RPM)

    - Paint rollers - 100mm & 230mm

4.8 Application method / tools - Application of floor wall functions and corners, Schonox ST Sealing Tape and ST corners.
    - Apply the adhesive to the substrate junctions with a 100mm roller or v notched trowel. While the adhesive is wet embed the Schonox St Sealing Tape and corners in position
    - Apply addition adhesive to overlap the joins and smooth out with a plastic scraper
    (Cite: Data sheet page 4: Application Instructions > Application Method / Tools > Application of floor wall functions and corners, Schonox ST Sealing Tape and ST corners)
4.9 Application method / tools - Application of Pipe Penetrations - Schonox ST D components.
    - For PVC fittings, apply a single coat of SikaFloor-14 Prep n Prime and allow to dry.
    - For Metal Fittings, lightly abrade with steel wool and then clean residue with Isopropyl Alcohol and allow to dry. Then apply a single coat of SIkaFloor-14 Prep n Prime and allow to dry.
    - Apply the adhesive around the pipe penetration with the Schonox 100mm roller or a V notched trowel and embed the Schonox ST pipe penetrations and flatten with a plastic scrapper.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 4: Application Instructions > Application Method / Tools > Application of Pipe Penetrations - Schonox ST D components)
4.10 Application method / tools - Application of the Sconox Ab Sheet Membrane..
    - Measure and cut sheet as required with the Utility Knife or Scissors
    - Apply a layer of the adhesive with a roller or V notched trowel to an area that can be covered prior to adhesive skinning (approx 1m2) and embed the Shonox AB sheet into the adhesive. Immediately fatten the sheet with a plastic scraper. Repeat process by gradually rolling and apply adhesive as required. All sheet joins are to be overlapped by a minimum 50mm and ensure the adhesive covers all sheet joins for a watertight finish.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 4: Application Instructions > Application Method / Tools > Application of the Sconox Ab Sheet Membrane.)
4.11 Application method / tools - Waterstop Angles, and Terminations.
    - Penetration can be terminated onto the pipe penetrations. Prime PVC pipes with SikaFloor-14 Prep n Prime and allow to dry. Apply the adhesive to the primer and install the Schonox ST D pipe penetrations.
    - Control Leak flanges shall be primed with SikaFloor-14 Prep n Prime and allowed to dry prior to bonding sheet terminations or ST Sealing with the adhesive Waterstop Angles shall be primed with SikaFloor-14 Prep n Prime and allowed to dry. Bond the Schonox ST Sealing tape to Waterstop angles with the adhesive as needed.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 4: Application Instructions > Application Method / Tools > Waterstop Angles, and Terminations)
4.12 Application method / tools - Application Notes.
    - Ensure all sheet joins and tapes etc are overlapped by 50mm.
    - Ensure all sheet joins and over laps are well bonded. If required the adhesive can be skimmed over sheet joins to protect sheet edges.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 4: Application Instructions > Application Method / Tools > Application Notes)
4.13 Cleaning of equipment. Tools and equipment can be clears with water prior to the adhesive setting.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 4: Application Instructions > Cleaning Of Equipment)
4.14 Limitations and important considerations.
    - Not suitable for externally application over early aged concrete or screeds.
