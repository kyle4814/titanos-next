Engineered or agglomerate stone (not moisture sensitive) on timber or particleboard floor (over-sheeted): internal floor

This is the ordered tiling system that Sika Australia's published documentation supports for engineered or agglomerate stone (not moisture sensitive) laid on timber or particleboard floor (over-sheeted) in this location: internal floor (dry area). 3 of the 5 steps are stated in the documentation. Any step the documentation is silent on is marked as not stated, and it is never guessed.

Answers used
Tile
Engineered or agglomerate stone (not moisture sensitive)
Substrate
Timber or particleboard floor (over-sheeted)
Location
Internal floor (dry area)
  • Moisture sensitive stone is excluded by the adhesive text. “*Not suitable for use with tile and stone sensitive to moisture/water.” [source 1]

Left at 0 unless you add one. It is never applied silently.

  1. Step 1. Prepare and prime Not stated in the published documentation

    No primer is named for this combination in the documentation. Check the data sheet of the products below.

    Data sheet to check: SikaTile®-400 EVO PRO product data sheet (PDF)

  2. Step 2. Waterproofing membrane Not stated in the published documentation

    Not stated in the published documentation. This sheet adds a membrane step only for wet areas, external areas and pools.

    Data sheet to check: SikaTile®-400 EVO PRO product data sheet (PDF)

  3. Step 3. Tile adhesive Stated in the documentation

    • SikaTile®-400 EVO PRO SikaTile

      SikaTile®-400 EVO PRO is a flexible, cement-based tile adhesive designed for natural stone, porcelain and mosaics.

      Why it is here: Its documentation lists engineered or agglomerate stone (not moisture sensitive), timber or particleboard floor (over-sheeted) and internal floor.

      • Tile type: Suitable tile types include; “Engineered stone/Manmade stone”
      • Substrate: “Timber and particleboard flooring over-sheeted with ceramic tile underlay or a suitable reinforced screed”
      • Location: “SikaTile®-400 EVO PRO is suitable for internal and external applications.”
      • Location: “Commercial and domestic walls and floors including high foot traffic locations, bathrooms, showers, kitchens, laundries, living rooms, balconies, patios, swimming pools and spas.”

      Check: “*Not suitable for use with tile and stone sensitive to moisture/water.”

      Published coverage: 20kg Bag of SikaTile®-400 EVO PRO covers approximately 6-7m2 with a 10mm notched trowel.

  4. Step 4. Grout Stated in the documentation

    • SikaCeram®-690 Elite Sika

      SikaCeram®-690 Elite is a high performance cementitious, fast setting, wall / floor tile grout for use in joints from 1 mm to 10 mm in width.

      Why it is here: Its documentation lists engineered or agglomerate stone (not moisture sensitive) and suits internal floor. Joint width: 1 to 10 mm.

      • Tile type: Grouting the following tile types : “Artificial stone tiles”
      • Location: “For indoor and outdoor use including pools.”
      • Location: “A high performance cementitious wall / floor tile grout for joints 1 mm to 10 mm.”
      • Joint width: “Grout joint range: 1-10 mm”

      Published coverage: Quanity of grout required per kg/m2 per joint width.
