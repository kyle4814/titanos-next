3 SUBSTRATE PREPARATION
3.1 General. Prepare substrates in accordance with the manufacturer's Product Data Sheet (September 2026, Version 02.01) and the requirements below. Inspect the substrate against these requirements before application.
3.2 Substrate quality.
    - All surfaces must be installed according to manufacturer’s instructions and relevant Australian Standard(s) and be structurally sound, dry, clean and free from movement, oil, grease, wax, curing compounds, release agents and any other loose or contaminating material.
    - Prior to application, remove all sharp protrusions, which may pierce the membrane
    - Any voids, potholes in the substrate must be appropriately filled up with a high strength mortar refer to Sika Monotop® mortars.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality)
3.3 Substrate quality - Concrete.
    - All new concrete slabs must have a wood float finish and be allowed to cure for 28 days. (refer to priming details for early aged concrete)
    - Old concrete must be cleaned with a strong commercial grade detergent or degreaser. Residue must then be thoroughly washed off with clean water. Allow the surface to dry for at least 24 hours
    - All laitance and efflorescence must be removed prior to application.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > Concrete)
3.4 Substrate quality - Cement based Renders & Screeds.
    - Renders and screeds should have a wood float finish and be allow to cure for 5-7. (refer priming details for early aged green screeds)
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > Cement based Renders & Screeds)
3.5 Substrate quality - Cement Boards and Sheets.
    - All boards and sheet shall be installed in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions and be specifically designed for tiling applications.
    - Screw and nail heads must be treated with a Sika® Neutral cure silicone or Sika® Fillet Joint.
    - All sheet joints, seams, penetrations, and wall floor junctions shall have a Sika® Neutral cure silicone, Sikaflex® Fillet Joint or Sika SealTape F flexible waterproofing bandage.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > Cement Boards and Sheets)
3.6 Substrate preparation - Substrate Priming Chart.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Preparation > Substrate Priming Chart)
3.7 Substrate preparation - Early aged (green) screeds Sikalastic® Moisture Seal.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Preparation > Early aged (green) screeds Sikalastic® Moisture Seal)
3.8 Substrate preparation - Static Cracks - not subject to movement.
    - Small hairline cracks, up to 1mm wide, may be filled by the first application of SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof
    - For cracks / joints wider than 1mm, a joint filler should be applied along the length of the crack prior to the application of SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Preparation > Static Cracks - not subject to movement)
3.9 Substrate preparation - Bond Breaker. SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof has high extensibility and is designed for use with a 12 mm bond breaker, a bead of Sika® Neutral cure silicone, Sika® Fillet Joint or a suitable Sika® flexible waterproofing bandage. The sealant applied must be tooled off to form a 12 mm wide bond breaker. A bond breaker must be installed at areas subject to movement, wall/wall junction, wall/floor junction, sheet joints and seams, penetrations and where there is a change in the direction or substrate type.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Preparation > Bond Breaker)
3.10 Substrate preparation - Falls to Drains. All areas shall have falls in accordance with National Construction Code (NCC) requirements.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Preparation > Falls to Drains)

4 APPLICATION
4.1 General. Apply in accordance with the manufacturer's Product Data Sheet (September 2026, Version 02.01) and the requirements below.
4.2 Application data.
    - Consumption: The minimum dry film thickness required is 1.0mm applied in a minimum 2 coat installation. A 15 Litre pail will cover 10m²
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Information)
4.3 Applied product ready for use. Drying Time
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Information > Applied product ready for use)
4.4 Equipment.
    - Brush, or short nap roller
    - Protective personal equipment
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Instructions > Equipment)
4.5 Application method / tools - Standard Application.
    - 1. SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof requires no mixing. Apply directly from the pail. Use a brush or roller to apply the first coat of SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof on the area to be waterproofed. 2. Allow the first coat to dry for approximately 2- 3 hours before applying the 2nd coat at 90° to the first coat. Ensure there are no imperfections on the waterproofing membrane. 3. Apply a third coat if necessary or required to do so. The minimum dry film thinkness shall be 1.00mm 4. Allow the final coat to dry for at least 8 hours prior to tiling application
    - Application under Pedestall & Paver application 1. SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof requires no mixing. Apply directly from the pail. Use a brush or roller to apply the first coat of SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof on the area to be waterproofed. 2. Allow the first coat to dry for approximately 2-3 hours before applying the 2nd coat at 90° to the first coat. Ensure there are no imperfections on the waterproofing membrane. 3. Apply a third coatas (as additional wear layer for pedestall and paver application. 4. The minimum dry film thickness for under pedestall and paver shall be 1.6mm 5. Allow the final coat to dry for at least 24 hours prior to pedestall and paver application
    - Drying times are influence by factors beyond the control of the manufacturer, use drying times as a guide only.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Application Method / Tools > Standard Application)
4.6 Application method / tools - Flood testing (if required).
    - Allow SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof to dry and cure for a minimum 72 hours at 23°C and 50% relative humidity for standard applications, and 96 hours at 23°C and 50% relative humidity for paver and pedestall application.
    - Conduct flood test for 24 hours at max 50mm depth with water.
    - Inspect membrane for water loss, release water and allow area to dry prior prior to subsequent coatings
    - Repair membrane if required.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Application Method / Tools > Flood testing (if required))
4.7 Cleaning of equipment. Clean all tools and application equipment immediately after us with water. Once cured, residual material can only be removed mechanically.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Cleaning Of Equipment)
4.8 Limitations and important considerations.
    - SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof is not suitable for applications in areas of permanent water immersion.
    - SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof is not suitable for applications in area of negative hydrostatic pressure or rising damp. Seek sika technical advice.
    - Delay applications if rain or inclement weather is imminent.
    - SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof must be applied at the recommended coverage rates.
    - Tongue & groove timber flooring must be over sheeted with fibre cement sheeting or ceramic tile underlay.
    - Partical board flooring shall be adequately prepared with all contaminates removed and over sheeted with a suitable ceramic tile underlay or reinforced screed.
    - To minimise the chance of damage install finished covering as soon as possible once the membrane has cured.
    - SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof must not be used as a wearing surface for foot and vehicular traffic.
    - Contact Sika® for information or applications not mentioned in the document.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Important Considerations)

Data sheet edition: June 2026, Version 01.07

1 SCOPE
1.1 Work included. Provide Davco® K10 Plus where documented, installed or applied in accordance with this clause and the manufacturer's Product Data Sheet (June 2026, Version 01.07).
1.2 Product description. Davco® K10 Plus is a premium performance, flexible, premixed, ready to use, water based polyurethane Class III waterproofing membrane designed for use under tile and stone and other resilient finishes.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 1: Description)
1.3 Intended uses stated by the manufacturer. Davco® K10 Plus is suitable for interior and exterior applications. Commercial and domestic wall and floors including wet areas such as showers bathrooms, terraces, balconies, roof and walkways prior to tiling, using a Sika branded polymer modified cement based tile adhesive. Davco® K10 Plus is also suitable for confined areas as it is water based and solvent free.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 1: Uses)
1.4 Limits of use. Observe the manufacturer's limitations stated in clause 4.6.

3 SUBSTRATE PREPARATION
3.1 General. Prepare substrates in accordance with the manufacturer's Product Data Sheet (June 2026, Version 01.07) and the requirements below. Inspect the substrate against these requirements before application.
3.2 Substrate quality - General.
    - All surfaces must be installed according to manufacturer's instructions and relevant Australian Standards and be structurally sound, dry, clean and free from movement, oil, grease, wax, curing compounds, release agents, efflorescence or any other loose or contaminating material.
    - Prior to application, remove all sharp protrusions, which may pierce the membrane
    - Avoid voids, potholes and divots in the substrate must be appropriately prepared with a Sika branded patching mortar.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > General)
3.3 Substrate quality - Concrete.
    - All new concrete slabs must have a wood float finish and be allowed to cure for 6 weeks.
    - Old concrete must be cleaned with a strong commercial grade detergent or degreaser. Residue must be thoroughly washed and rinse with clean water after cleaning. Allow the surface to dry for at least 24 hours.
    - If the concrete has steel trowel or power float finish, it must be mechanically prepared to expose the aggregate. Signs of Laitance and Efflorescence must be removed.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > Concrete)
3.4 Substrate quality - Cement Render & Screeds.
    - Rendered or screeds must have a wood float finish and be allowed to cure for 7 days.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > Cement Render & Screeds)
3.5 Substrate quality - Light Weight Blocks.
    - Apply 2 coats of a suitable Sika branded porous primer, allow to dry between coats.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > Light Weight Blocks)
3.6 Substrate quality - Metal Surfaces.
    - All metal surfaces must be free from rust
    - Prime metal surface with a suitable etching primer
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > Metal Surfaces)
3.7 Substrate quality - Crack / Joints - subject to movement.
    - All cracks / joints, irrespective of their width, must be filled with a Sika branded Neutral cure silicone sealant. Then a 50mm wide polyethylene / polypropylene tape should be placed over the crack, ensuring it adheres to the surface.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > Crack / Joints - subject to movement)
3.8 Substrate quality - Building Boards and Sheeting.
    - Standard wall / floor building boards must be primed with a suitable Sika Branded primer and firmly fixed in accordance to manufacturer instructions and applicable Australian Standards. Such boards / sheeting include plasterboard, fibre cement sheeting, marine grade ply and wet area composition board. Check with the board / sheeting manufacturer for their suitability.
    - Screw or nail heads must be treated with a Sika branded epoxy or a Sika branded Neutral cure silicone sealant
    - All sheeting joints need to be covered with a 50mm wide Polyethylene / Polypropylene tape.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > Building Boards and Sheeting)
3.9 Substrate quality - Falls to Drains.
    - In all wet areas, it is important that falls be provided to the drain outlet. The slope of this fall should be 1:80 - which equates to 12.5mm fall over 1m. For wet areas, balconies, and rooftops, if the existing substrate does not provide the required falls, a screed will be need to be created. Once the falls are in place and screeds have cured apply the Davco® K10 Plus as per instructions. Contact Sika Australia for more information on screed mix should this be required.
    - For balconies and rooftops, the slope of this fall should be 1:100 - which equates to a 10mm fall over 1m. If the existing substrate does not provide the necessary fall, a sand / cement screed needs to be created. Once the screed is in place and has cured adequately, apply the membrane as per instructions below. Contact Sika Australia for more information on an appropriate screed mix should this be required.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality > Falls to Drains)
3.10 Substrate quality / pre-treatment. Surfaces that are to receive Davco® K10 Plus shall be primed with a suitable Sika primer prior to the waterproofing membrane application.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality / Pre-Treatment)
3.11 Substrate quality / pre-treatment - Primer Guide.
    - Refer to the relevant specific primer product data sheet for complete user instructions prior to use.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Substrate Quality / Pre-Treatment > Primer Guide)

4 APPLICATION
4.1 General. Apply in accordance with the manufacturer's Product Data Sheet (June 2026, Version 01.07) and the requirements below.
4.2 Consumption. Davco® K10 Plus is designed to be applied to substrates in a minimum 2 coat application to acheive a 1mm dry film thickness. Each coat shall be applied at a minimum thickness 0.75mm per coat at wet film thickness. Apply additional coats to acheive the minimum 1mm dry film requirements. A 20Ltr pail will cover approximately 13.3m2 with 2 coats applied. A 4Ltr pail will cover approximately 2.75m2 with 2 coats applied.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Information > Consumption)
4.3 Drying Time.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Information > Drying Time)
4.4 Application.
    - Ensure surafces are adequatley prepared, clean and dry, free from bond inhibitors and contaminates
    - Use a thick brush or a medium nap roller to apply the first coat of Davco® K10 Plus on the area to be waterproofed
    - Allow the first coat to dry for approximately 1-2 hours before applying the 2nd coat at 90° to the first coat. Ensure there are no pinholes or air bubbles on the membrane surface.
    - Apply a third coat only if necessary or required to do so
    - Allow the final coat to dry for at least 6 hours before tiling (according to temperature conditions). This gives an overall drying time of 6-8 hours for the full application.
    - Ensure final dried thicknes of the waterproofing membrane is minimum 1.0mm.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Application)
4.5 Cleaning of tools.
    - Tools and excess K10 Plus can be cleaned up with water while it is still wet
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Cleaning Of Tools)
4.6 Limitations and important considerations.
    - Do not apply in temperatures above 35°C or below 5°C
    - Do not allow the product to freeze
    - Delay external applications when inclement weather is imminent
    - Do not thin the liquid, product is ready to use.
    - Do not use in areas of permanent immersion such as swimming pools or ponds etc
    - Do not use where negative hydrostatic pressure is evident (rising damp), as if affects the bond of Davco® K10 Plus. Contact Sika technical for further advice.
    - Substrates shall be rigid enough to accommodate the incumbent tile, stone or other finishing material and comply with applicable deflection standards.
    - For other uses not mentioned in this document please consult Sika Australia.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Important Considerations)
