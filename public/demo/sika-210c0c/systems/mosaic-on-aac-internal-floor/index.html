Mosaic tiles on autoclaved aerated concrete (aac): internal floor

This is the ordered tiling system that Sika Australia's published documentation supports for mosaic tiles laid on autoclaved aerated concrete (aac) in this location: internal floor (dry area). 4 of the 5 steps are stated in the documentation. Any step the documentation is silent on is marked as not stated, and it is never guessed.

Mosaic tiles
Substrate
Autoclaved aerated concrete (AAC)
