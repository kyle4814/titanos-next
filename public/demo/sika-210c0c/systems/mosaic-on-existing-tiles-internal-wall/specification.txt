4 APPLICATION
4.1 General. Apply in accordance with the manufacturer's Product Data Sheet (May 2026, Version 01.01) and the requirements below.
4.2 Application data.
    - Mixing ratio: 5.5 - 6 Litres of clean cool potable water per 15kg bag
    - Consumption: 7.5m² with a 10mm notched trowel; Coverage is dependant of surface profile and application technique.
    - Pot Life: 2 hours at 23° degrees and 50% relative humidity. Adhesive may need restirring during its pot life. Restir without adding any additional water.
    - Open Time: 30 minutes at 23°C degrees and 50% relative humidity
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Information)
4.3 Applied product ready for use.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Information > Applied product ready for use)
4.4 Equipment.
    - Personal protective equipment
    - Mixing drill with stirrer attachment
    - Clean mixing buckets
    - Trowels
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Application Instructions > Equipment)
4.5 Mixing.
    - Measure recommended amount of clean cool potable water and slowing add the SikaTile®-305 Master Lite powder. Gradually stir the mixture untill it reaches a smooth lump free consistency (approximately 2 minutes).
    - Allow the mixture to stand or "slake" for 3-5 minutes then restir for an additional minute without add any aditional water.
    - Restiring during the adhesives pot life may be required, do not add any additional water, restir only.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Mixing)
4.6 Application.
    - Tiles shall be fixed in accordance with Australian Standards.
    - Ensure the back of tiles are from dust and contaminates.
    - Apply the adhesive with a notched trowel to the substrate to achieve minimum coverage requirement outlined in the relevant Australian Standards.
    - Adhesive notches should be applied in a single direction.
    - Apply tiles into the wet adhesive and break the adhesive notches to fully bed the tile in the adhesive layer. Ensure adequate coverage outlined in the Australlian Standards is achieved to both substrate and the back of tiles.
    - Tiles 400x400 and larger shall be back buttered as per relevant Australian Standards.
    - Clean excess adhesive out of the grout joints while the adhesive is still wet.
    - Allow the adhesive to dry and set prior to grouting.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Application)
4.7 Cleaning of equipment.
    - Tools and equipment can be cleaned with water prior to the adhesive setting.
    - Adhesive that has set will require mechanical removal.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 3: Application Instructions > Cleaning Of Equipment)
4.8 Limitations and important considerations.
    - Test product in an inconspicuous area first
    - Do not apply in temperatures above 35ºC or below 5ºC
    - Do not allow the adhesive to skin prior to laying the tiles. If the adhesive skins remove and dispose of it, then re-apply fresh adhesive
    - Not suitable for moisture sensitive stone, seek further advice from Sika technical support.
    - Not suitable for use for direct application over timber substrates. Apply a suitable screed or ceramic tile underlay over timber substrates.
    - Due to external influences efflorescence is beyond the control of the manufacturer
    - Setting times of the adhesive can be affected by site conditions, climate and could be faster or slower than specified.
    - Not suitable for applications externally above 3 meters.
    - Not suitable for fixing resin backed tile and stone. Prime resin back tiles with SikaFloor-14 Prep n prime prior to fixing, and refer to product information and limitations on the product data sheet
    - Contact Sika Australia for uses not mentioned in this document.
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Limitations Of Use)
4.9 Further documentation. It is essential to plan the area to be tiled, with the inclusion of movement joints, in accordance with the relevant Australian Standard AS3958:2023 . Failure to do so may result in drummy or lifting tiles. Make sure normal building or surface movement is accommodated by installing movement joints as follows:
    - Over movement joints in the substrate
    - At a junction between different substrate materials
    - At fixtures interrupting the tile surface e.g. columns
    - At internal vertical corners
    - Around the perimeter of the tiling system
    - At a maximum of 4.5m centres, in a grid pattern
    - Movement joints should be kept free from dirt and adhesive
    - All movement joints can be filled with a suitable Sika sealant and suitable backing rod or backing tape
    (Cite: Data sheet page 2: Further Documentation)
