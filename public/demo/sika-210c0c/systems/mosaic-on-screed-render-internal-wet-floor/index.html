Mosaic tiles on cement screed or cement render: internal wet area floor

This is the ordered tiling system that Sika Australia's published documentation supports for mosaic tiles laid on cement screed or cement render in this location: internal wet area floor (bathroom, shower, laundry). 5 of the 5 steps are stated in the documentation. Any step the documentation is silent on is marked as not stated, and it is never guessed.

Mosaic tiles

  2. Step 2. Waterproofing membrane Stated in the documentation

    • Davco® K10 Rapid Davco

      Davco® K10 Rapid is a flexible, rapid drying, Class III waterproofing membrane designed for internal and external applications.

      Why it is here: Its documentation says it goes under tiles and lists cement screed or cement render as a substrate.

      • Substrate: “Most common substrates; concrete, cement renders, screed, lightweight blocks, prepared metal and pvc control leak flanges, building boards approved for wet areas, such as compressed fibre cement sheeting.”
      • Location: “Davco® K10 Rapid is a flexible, rapid drying, Class III waterproofing membrane designed for internal and external applications.”
      • Location: “Commercial and domestic walls and floors including wet areas such as showers, bathrooms, terraces, balconies, roofs walkways prior to tiling, using Davco cement based adhesives.”

      Published coverage: Davco® K10 Rapid is designed to be applied to substrates in a minimum 2 coat application to acheive a 1mm dry film thickness.
      Each coat shall be applied at a minimum thickness 0.75mm per coat at wet film thickness.
      Apply additional coats to acheive the minimum 1mm dry film requirements.
      A 15Ltr pail will cover approximately 10m2 with 2 coats.
