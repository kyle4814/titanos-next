Quarry, terracotta or slate tiles on fibre cement sheet or cement board: internal wet area floor

This is the ordered tiling system that Sika Australia's published documentation supports for quarry, terracotta or slate tiles laid on fibre cement sheet or cement board in this location: internal wet area floor (bathroom, shower, laundry). 4 of the 5 steps are stated in the documentation. Any step the documentation is silent on is marked as not stated, and it is never guessed.
