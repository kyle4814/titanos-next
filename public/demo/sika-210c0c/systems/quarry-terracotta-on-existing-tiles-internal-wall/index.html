Quarry, terracotta or slate tiles on existing tiles (tile over tile): internal wall

This is the ordered tiling system that Sika Australia's published documentation supports for quarry, terracotta or slate tiles laid on existing tiles (tile over tile) in this location: internal wall (dry area). 4 of the 5 steps are stated in the documentation. Any step the documentation is silent on is marked as not stated, and it is never guessed.

The system, step by step

  1. Step 1. Prepare and prime Stated in the documentation

    • Sikafloor®-14 Prep N Prime Impact Sika

      Sikafloor®-14 Prep N Prime Impact is a specialty solvent free, ready to use non porous primer designed for use over smooth and non-porous surfaces and substrates.

      Why it is here: The documentation names this primer for existing tiles (tile over tile).

      • Primer named in a data sheet: “Non-porous substrates and Tile on tile | Sikafloor-14 Prep n Prime Impact”

      Published coverage: 8-10m2 per lite dependant on substrate texture and profile.

      The published coverage does not say what quantity it is per, so no amount is worked out here. Check the data sheet.

      Pack sizes: 10 l, 4 l

      Product data sheet (PDF) Safety data sheet (PDF) Company page [source 1]

    From the documentation: “Ensure all existing tiles are firmly fixed, loose or hollow tiles must be removed and the area repaired using a suitable Sika repair mortar. Existing tiles shall be adequately cleaned prior to the application of the primer. Existing tiles shall be primed with Sikafloor-14 Prep N Prime Impact and allowed to dry prior to tiling.” [source 2]
