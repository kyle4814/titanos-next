Quarry, terracotta or slate tiles on plasterboard: external floor

This is the ordered tiling system that Sika Australia's published documentation supports for quarry, terracotta or slate tiles laid on plasterboard in this location: external floor (balcony, terrace, patio). 4 of the 5 steps are stated in the documentation. Any step the documentation is silent on is marked as not stated, and it is never guessed.

Answers used
Tile
Quarry, terracotta or slate tiles
Substrate
Plasterboard
Location
External floor (balcony, terrace, patio)

    • SikaTile®-010 Secure Prime SikaTile

      SikaTile®-010 Secure Prime is a high performance, ready to use, primer with efflorescence inhibiting properties suitable for porous and dense substrates.

      Why it is here: The documentation names this primer for plasterboard.

      • Primer substrate: Suitable substrates include. “Plasterboard”

      Published coverage: Approximately 13 m2 per litre dependant on substrate porosity.

  2. Step 2. Waterproofing membrane Stated in the documentation

    • SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof SikaTile

      A premium performance, flexible class III, ready to use, water based polyurethane waterproofing membrane designed for internal and external areas including wet areas, balconies, terraces, and non UV exposed above ground external applications.

      Why it is here: Its documentation says it goes under tiles and lists plasterboard as a substrate.

      • Substrate: “Plasterboard”
      • Location: “A premium performance, flexible class III, ready to use, water based polyurethane waterproofing membrane designed for internal and external areas including wet areas, balconies, terraces, and non UV exposed above ground external applications.”
      • Location: “SikaTile®-110 Secure Proof is suitable for use on walls and floors for both internal and external applications including under pedistall and paver applications.”

      Published coverage: The minimum dry film thickness required is 1.0mm applied in a minimum 2 coat installation.
      A 15 Litre pail will cover 10m2

      A crack bridging, waterproof sheet membrane system made of polyethylene covered with a special fleece designed for use in wet areas, balconies and swimming pools.

      Why it is here: Its documentation says it goes under tiles and lists plasterboard as a substrate.

      • Substrate: Substrates Schonox AB is suitable on “Gypsum Plaster Boards*”
      • Location: “A crack bridging, waterproof sheet membrane system made of polyethylene covered with a special fleece designed for use in wet areas, balconies and swimming pools.”
      • Location: “Extra Heavy Duty - Robinson floor test”

      No rate is published for this product. Check the data sheet.

    • Davco® K10 Plus Davco

      Davco® K10 Plus is a premium performance, flexible, premixed, ready to use, water based polyurethane Class III waterproofing membrane designed for use under tile and stone and other resilient finishes.

      Why it is here: Its documentation says it goes under tiles. It does not list substrates, so check the data sheet for yours.

      • Location: “Davco® K10 Plus is suitable for interior and exterior applications.”
      • Location: “Commercial and domestic wall and floors including wet areas such as showers bathrooms, terraces, balconies, roof and walkways prior to tiling, using a Sika branded polymer modified cement based tile adhesive.”

      Published coverage: Davco® K10 Plus is designed to be applied to substrates in a minimum 2 coat application to acheive a 1mm dry film thickness.
      Each coat shall be applied at a minimum thickness 0.75mm per coat at wet film thickness.
      Apply additional coats to acheive the minimum 1mm dry film requirements.
      A 20Ltr pail will cover approximately 13.3m2 with 2 coats applied.
      A 4Ltr pail will cover approximately 2.75m2 with 2 coats applied.
