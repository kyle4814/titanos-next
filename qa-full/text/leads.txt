06 / 07
Using the List the Right Way

The Privacy Act covers how I built the list. The Spam Act 2003 covers how you use it. Three rules keep you clean: B2B outreach to published business addresses relies on inferred consent, which is exactly why I verify roles and sources instead of scraping personal inboxes. Every send needs your real business name and contact details. Every send needs a working unsubscribe, honoured within 5 business days. Follow those and cold B2B email is legal in Australia. I build the list to make the first rule true. The other two are on your sender. If you'd rather not think about any of it, that's what the managed retainer is for.
