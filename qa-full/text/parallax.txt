The simplest way to bring AI into a large organisation.

Nothing replaced, nothing to learn, nothing to install. You do three things. We do everything else, beside the systems you already run.

Your part: three things
1
One conversation. Tell us where the hours go. That is the whole brief.
2
One approval. You choose how much we see, starting from nothing at all.
3
One tap per decision. Anything that sends, spends or changes something waits for your yes.
Our part: everything else

Finding where the hours go, building the system, testing it end to end, running it, and reporting what it gave back. It is 99% code and 1% AI, so it runs for about AU$20 to AU$50 a month on your own accounts, with nothing marked up. See the costs and the proof.

Go as deep as you like, one step at a time
Level 0: Nothing connected
We work from public records and your filed accounts. You get the analysis before you share a thing.
Level 1: Forward and share
Forward an email or share one spreadsheet. We return the work done.
Level 2: Read-only, one system
Read-only access to one system you choose. Nothing in it can be changed by us.
Level 3: Approved actions
The system drafts and prepares; a person on your side taps yes before anything happens.

Most organisations start at Level 0 and only move up once the numbers have earned it. Stopping at any level is fine.

What we never do
Replace your systems. We sit beside what you already run.
Replace your people. The repetitive work moves to the machine, and their hours go back to the work only they can do.
Ask your team to learn new software. They keep the tools they know.
Take control from your IT team. They stay in charge, and every action we take is logged for them to check.
Act without a yes. Every action that matters waits for one tap.
