Construction

Builders and trades lose money to fake invoices and payment redirects, and the cause is usually one missing email setting.

Councils

Local government buys locally and publishes how, so the first job is finding the door that fits you.

Health

A clinic holds the most private data a small business can hold, so a calm review of the basics comes first.

Aged care and disability

Providers are expected to show they follow a recognised security standard, and many have never been asked how.

Logistics

Freight runs on bookings, invoices and bank details sent by email, which is where a forged message does the most harm.

Mining and resources

Suppliers to resource companies are asked to prove their security before they are paid, and the proof can be prepared early.

Education

Schools hold family and child data and are rarely asked how their email is protected.

Retail

A shop's brand is its email and web address, so a lookalike or forged message costs trust as well as money.

Hospitality

Bookings, supplier invoices and staff rosters all live in email, and one forged message can redirect a payment.

Professional services

Advisers hold client money and client secrets, so clients quietly judge them on how safely they handle email.
