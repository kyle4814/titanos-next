FREE WORKFLOW X-RAY

Find the bottleneck costing your business time

Answer a few questions about one recurring task. You get a free bottleneck check, practical fixes and an improvement estimate you can inspect.

No account or system access needed to start. It runs in this page, on your device.

THREE PROMISES BEFORE YOU START

  • No login. You do not give an email address to see the result or download the report.
  • No software connection and no uploads. You describe the task in your own words and numbers.
  • The advice is yours to use whether or not you ever talk to us. Doing nothing is an allowed answer.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your answers are worked out inside this page, on your device. Nothing is sent, saved on our side, or scanned. There is no login. A draft is kept in this tab only so a refresh does not lose your place, and it disappears when you close the tab or press Clear my answers.

WHAT THE ESTIMATE IS, AND IS NOT

The hours are a scenario built from your own answers and a short list of labelled assumptions: how much of the hands-on time a tool could take off the task, how many people would really use it, and the upkeep it needs. Low, base and high are bands, not statistical ranges. Capacity freed is not cash saved, and waiting time is shown beside the hours, never added to them. If your answers are too thin, the X-Ray says so and gives you a small measuring exercise instead of a number.

It looks at the work, never at the people doing it. Nobody is assessed and nobody is replaced.

WHAT THIS IS NOT

This is a questionnaire. It does not read your documents, connect to your software, measure a pilot, or scan your website or network. Those are separate things, each with its own consent, and none of them is part of this free check. If you want a public email security check on your domain, that is the free scan, which is a different tool.

IF YOU WANT TO GO FURTHER

Only if you choose to. You can try the synthetic example above to see what a finished X-Ray looks like with fictional numbers, or talk to Kyle about a small measured trial. Nothing here signs you up to anything, and nothing is sent until you reach out yourself.
